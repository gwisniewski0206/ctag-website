LiteSolder is an extension to [LitePlacer](https://www.liteplacer.com/) which can dispense solderpaste with high accuracy.

Features:

-   Automatically dispense solderpaste on rectangular and circular PCB pads
-   GUI of LitePlacer now has an additional mode for dispensing solderpaste (motor setup for dispensing mode)
-   Dispensing solderpaste works with 15 cm spindle, 5 cm³ standard cartridge and steppermotor
-   Toggle between solder and place mode with a switch – no additional driver needed
-   Calibration of dispense nozzle for exact results
-   Loads a .gbr file and automatically find pad positions

The project is completely open source and available at [https://github.com/Carinalo93/LiteSolder/wiki](https://github.com/Carinalo93/LiteSolder/wiki) as well as [https://hackaday.io/project/164010-litesolder-solderpaste-dispenser](https://hackaday.io/project/164010-litesolder-solderpaste-dispenser).

The project would like to acknowledge the support by [Interreg MakerStartups](https://www.interreg5a.eu/blog/maker-startups/), enabling the evaluation of different solder pastes.
